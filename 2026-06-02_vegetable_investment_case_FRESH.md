### 3.3 Room B — Baby Leafy Greens

| Parameter | Spec |
|---|---|
| Floor area | 60 m² (5m × 12m) |
| Racks | 5-tier vertical system (estimate ~195 m² effective growing surface at 65% efficiency) ⚠️ |
| Growing height per tier | 65 cm pitch |
| LED clearance from canopy | 20 cm minimum |
| Target PPFD | ~450 µmol/m²/s (research spec for leafy green vegetative growth) ⚠️ |
| LED power intensity | 180 W/m² (calculated to achieve target PPFD with RBW spectrum) ⚠️ |
| Total LED power draw | 195 m² × 180 W = **35.1 kW** ⚠️ |
| Photoperiod | 16 hours/day |

**Temperature & Humidity:**
| Parameter | Spec |
|---|---|
| Temperature | 18–22°C (run at 20°C) ⚠️ |
| Humidity | 65–75% RH, with dehumidification for transpiration load ⚠️ |

**Sensible heat load (LED + transpiration + conduction):**
LED heat = 35.1 kW + plant transpiration (~5 kW latent converted) + wall heat ~2 kW = **~42 kW total load**

Divided into: **~23 kW sensible + ~19 kW latent**

**HVAC Option (requires formal quote):**
- 3× Mitsubishi Electric **PEY-SM42JA(L)** (12.3 kW each, SEER 19, R32) = **36.9 kW total capacity**
- COP ~2.8 at 20°C: Electrical draw = 12.3 kW ÷ 2.8 = **4.39 kW per unit × 3 = 13.2 kW electrical draw**
- **No standalone dehumidifier required.** 3 units provide ~6.3 kW latent capacity vs ~0.9 kW transpiration + evaporation load (7:1 surplus). Dehumidification handled by AC coil condensation at 10–12°C (below 13.9°C dew point at 20°C/70% RH).
- Configuration: All 3 units active ⚠️ Requires formal quote from KYW Thailand (02-763-7000)
- **David (2026-06-02):** 2-unit spec was undersized — 24.6 kW vs 33.4 kW sensible LED load = ~9 kW deficit. Third unit brings total to 36.9 kW with ~3.5 kW headroom. Transpiration load (~0.60 kW) is only ~1.7% of total LED input — typical for closed PFAL (Kozai et al.).

**Common error (David ERR-D01):** Forgetting dehumidification for Room B transpiration load. ❌ **This room REQUIRES both AC (sensible) and dehumidifier (latent).** Do not size AC alone.

## 10. Key Risks

1. **Electricity cost overrun / MEA tariff change** — Total draw ~33,348 kWh/month at ~62 kW peak. Modelled on MEA Type 3 non-TOU (฿3.571/kWh + ฿237/kW demand). Any tariff increase or higher-than-expected peak demand raises costs. **Mitigation:** Confirm Type 3 qualification with MEA before Phase 0; explore staggered LED startup to reduce peak demand charge; evaluate solar offset for LED load.

2. **Zoning blocks project** — Thai factory zoning may prohibit food production. **Mitigation:** Obtain legal opinion from Thai lawyer before Phase 0 spend (non-negotiable; currently Open Action #3).

3. **LED thermal management failure** — Inadequate cooling = canopy temperature stress + LED degradation (efficiency drops 5–7% per °C above spec). **Mitigation:** Over-spec HVAC capacity, test with Load cells in pilot phase.

4. **Hydroponic contamination — Pythium, algae, root rot** — Can wipe 100% of a room's harvest in 3–5 days. **Mitigation:** Daily water sampling, preventive sterilisation of trays/channels, backup nutrient recipe, early detection via plant appearance (yellowing, wilting).

5. **Yield per m² lower than research estimates** — No Thai commercial reference for this exact crop+environment mix. **Mitigation:** Conduct 10 m² pilot in Month 5 (Room A); validate yield before committing to Room B capex.

6. **Market development lag — pricing drops before buyers secured** — If wholesale prices fall during build phase, payback extends significantly. **Mitigation:** Lock in B2B buyer LOIs and pricing before Phase 0 spend (Open Action #2).

7. **CO₂ safety — high concentration hazard** — >5,000 ppm = respiratory hazard. **Mitigation:** Install CO₂ detector + alarm, enforce no-entry protocol above 2,000 ppm, ventilation SOP, annual safety audit.

8. **Grid reliability — blackout = crop loss** — Bangkok grid outages (hours to days) = plant death if no cooling. **Mitigation:** Generator (capex ฿50–100k) non-negotiable for Room B (cold-intolerant crop), optional for Room A. Evaluate in risk analysis.

9. **Lead Grower recruitment / retention** — Wrong hire = contamination + yield loss. **Mitigation:** Recruit from existing Thai vertical farm operator; include 3-month onboarding + performance guarantees in contract.

10. **Supplier dependency on Civic Agrotech LED panels** — Single-source risk at ฿21,500/unit. Price increase or unavailability kills CapEx plan. **Mitigation:** Obtain 2–3 competing quotes for RBW LED panels before Phase 0 lock-in.
