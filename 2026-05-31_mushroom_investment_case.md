## 1.1 Staffing Detail

### Role 1 — Lead Cultivation Technician
**Salary: ฿25,000–30,000/month**
Source: JobsDB Bangkok employer-disclosed lab technician average ฿24,999/month (2024–2025). Candidate with verified commercial mushroom cultivation experience may command the upper end.

This is the most critical hire. Everything that determines whether the operation produces clean mushrooms or contaminated bags runs through this person.

**Daily responsibilities:**
- All inoculation under the laminar flow hood (~3 hours/day — sterile technique, no errors)
- Spawn stock management — ordering, quality inspection, storage conditions
- Moving colonised blocks from colonisation room to fruiting rooms at the right time
- Scoring LM bags on entry to fruiting room (cutting to expose surface for pinning)
- Contamination diagnosis — identifying problems early before they spread to adjacent blocks
- Monitoring colonisation room and fruiting room environment (temperature, humidity, CO₂ log)
- Autoclave operation, cycle verification, and safety monitoring
- Troubleshooting yield or quality issues

**What this person must know before starting:**
Commercial mushroom growing at scale — inoculation technique, sterile discipline, contamination identification by sight. A candidate who has worked at a Thai commercial mushroom operation (e.g., Earthling Mushroom or equivalent) for 2+ years is the target profile. A training placement at a working Thai commercial LM farm before the operation opens is strongly recommended and will reduce the contamination risk in the critical early months.

**What they do NOT need:**
A degree in microbiology. Practical experience outweighs academic background for this role.

### Station 4 — Colonisation Room (21–24°C, both species)

Both LM and Nameko mycelium require **21–24°C** for healthy spawn run. Bangkok ambient (28–35°C) exceeds the safe upper limit — above 27°C, contamination accelerates faster than mycelium growth.

**Sources:**
- LM optimal spawn run: 21–24°C (multiple cultivation guides; Earthling Mushroom colonisation room confirmed at 23–24°C — clip 782294073, facility visit 2026-05-31)
- Nameko optimal spawn run: 21–24°C (Mushroology.com; The Spore Depot cultivation guide — researched 2026-05-31)

Bags are sealed during colonisation — no spores released, minimal contamination risk, tight stacking permitted (4–5 tiers). One shared room serves both species.

| Species | Colonisation time | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Lion's Mane | **21 days** | 14–21 day range at 21–24°C, multiple guides. Conservative end. |
| Nameko | **45 days** | 30–45 day range at 21–24°C, Mushroology.com / The Spore Depot. Conservative end. ⚠️ Verify with Thai Nameko grower. |

**Colonisation zone size required:**
- LM: 3,000 blocks/month × (21/30) = 2,100 blocks colonising simultaneously
- Nameko: 1,500 × (45/30) = 2,250 blocks
- Total: **4,350 blocks** — at 4-tier racking: ~22 m² floor space

## 3. Room Specifications

### 3.1 HVAC Design — Both Fruiting Rooms

Both rooms use a **concealed ducted split system** — corridor-mounted, supply-only.

```
CORRIDOR                          │ WALL │        FRUITING ROOM
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Concealed ducted AC unit ──► PIR supply duct ──► HEPA filter ──► cold air across shelves

Ultrasonic humidifier ─────► PP tubing ──────────────────────► distribution manifold

                                                 room air ──► filtered exhaust vent ──► corridor
```

- AC unit sits in corridor — no AC coil inside the fruiting room, zero spore contamination risk
- HEPA H14 filter at room-side duct inlet — final filtration before air reaches blocks
- Supply-only airflow — room runs at slight positive pressure, air exits through filtered exhaust only
- Humidity delivered separately via external ultrasonic humidifier, independent of temperature control

## 10. Key Risks

### R-01 — MEA electrical cost overrun
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium

Model uses ฿160,000 (MEA published midpoint, ฿85,000–฿235,000 range). Actual cost depends on transformer distance. ฿25,000 contingency reserve is built in. **Mitigation:** Complete MEA B-01 survey before any construction spend.

---

### R-02 — Zoning blocks the project
**Likelihood:** Low | **Impact:** Critical (existential)

Nong Khaem is mixed-use zoning. Food production in a factory may require a zoning variance or DoIW licence. **Mitigation:** Thai lawyer provides written zoning opinion in Week 1 — hard stop before any spend.

---

### R-03 — Nameko AC failure — crop lost within 2 hours
**Likelihood:** High (Bangkok heat) | **Impact:** High

Nameko fruits at 14–16°C in a city where ambient reaches 33–38°C. Any AC failure exceeding ~2 hours = crop loss. Single failure costs ฿126,600 in lost Nameko revenue plus wasted substrate. **Mitigation:** Two AC units (one active, one standby) + 25 kVA diesel generator with ATS. Both non-negotiable before Nameko room opens.

---

### R-04 — Autoclave quality (Option B)
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High

Chinese 200L autoclaves vary significantly between suppliers. A unit that cannot hold 121°C consistently will cause contamination across entire production batches before the problem is identified. **Mitigation:** Purchase only from a supplier who can provide a verified Thai reference user (another mushroom farm or laboratory). If no reference available, upgrade to Option A (HIRAYAMA, ฿284,505).

---

### R-05 — Contamination exceeds model assumptions
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High

Model uses 15% contamination for LM and 12% for Nameko. If sterile technique is poor, rates can climb above 25–30%, materially reducing output and revenue. At the 4,500-block production volume, contamination is the single largest operational risk. **Mitigation:** HEPA laminar flow hood for all inoculation, supply-only HVAC design, positive room pressure, and colonisation at correct temperature (21–24°C) are all control measures that must be maintained consistently.

---

### R-06 — Nameko colonisation start date missed
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High

Nameko requires 45 days colonisation before blocks can enter the fruiting room. If inoculation does not begin at Month 5 (when Phase 2 opens), the Nameko room sits empty and revenue is delayed. **Mitigation:** Nameko inoculation start date must be a hard milestone on the project schedule, triggered the day Phase 2 is deployed.

---

### R-07 — Yield-per-block lower than estimated
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium–High

LM first-flush yield (220g/block) and Nameko first-flush yield (160g/block) are research-derived estimates — neither has been confirmed with a Thai commercial grower. If LM yield is 160g/block (the PMC 2023 pure sawdust figure without bran supplement adjustment), monthly output drops from 561 kg to ~408 kg and revenue falls by ฿91,800/month, extending payback from 6 to ~8 months. **Mitigation:** Verify both figures with a Thai commercial grower before Phase 0 spend is committed.

---

### R-08 — Selling price falls below model assumption
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** Low

At ฿600/kg LM, the operation absorbs a **53% price decline** before breaking even. Even at Talad Thai commodity wholesale (฿450/kg), the operation generates ฿131,499/month profit — an 11-month payback. Price risk is low relative to the yield verification risk above. **Mitigation:** Secure B2B premium buyers before Phase 0 spend. Do not rely on spot Talad Thai prices as primary channel.
