# Phased Entry Scenario — Nong Khaem Factory
*Issued: 2026-05-31 | Companion to: 2026-05-31_mushroom_investment_case.md*

---

## Concept

Instead of building the full operation before generating any revenue, this scenario builds from the **output backwards**:

| Stage | What gets built | Where blocks come from |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | LM fruiting room only | Fully colonised blocks purchased from Thai supplier |
| **2** | + Colonisation room | Partially colonised blocks (inoculated by supplier, you finish colonisation) |
| **3** | + Full inoculation lab | 100% in-house production |
| **4** | + Nameko room | In-house Nameko blocks |

Revenue starts in Stage 1 as soon as blocks arrive. Each stage adds capability while reducing per-block cost. Capital is deployed in smaller increments, validated at each step before committing to the next.

## Stage 1 — Fruiting Room Only

### What you build
The LM fruiting room and the minimum pre-build infrastructure to run it. No autoclave, no laminar flow, no colonisation room.

### Where blocks come from
Fully colonised, ready-to-fruit Lion's Mane blocks purchased from a Thai commercial supplier. Earthling Mushroom (Nonthaburi) is the reference operation confirmed to produce LM at commercial scale. A supply agreement at 3,000 blocks/month is required before committing Stage 1 CapEx.

> ⚠️ **Critical pre-condition:** Confirm block supply price and availability with at least one Thai commercial LM supplier before building Stage 1. The financial model below uses ฿50/block — this is an estimate. The actual price must be confirmed before Stage 1 spend is committed.

### Stage 1 Staff (2 FTE)

**Lead Cultivation Technician (฿25,000/month)**
- Receive and inspect incoming colonised blocks
- Score bags and load fruiting room
- Monitor room conditions daily (temp, humidity, CO₂)
- Contamination triage — remove any compromised blocks immediately
- Harvest — first flush only, grade, discard block after harvest

**Production Worker (฿16,000/month)**
- Assist with harvesting
- Packaging, labelling, cold storage
- Clean fruiting room positions after harvest

## Stage 2 — Add Colonisation Room

### What changes
Build the colonisation room and add sterilisation + inoculation capability. Now you can receive **partially colonised blocks** from a supplier (already inoculated, 10+ days into colonisation) and complete the colonisation yourself. This reduces your per-block purchase cost.

Alternatively, if a supplier will provide **just-inoculated blocks** (sterilised substrate + inoculated by supplier, no colonisation time yet), you take over immediately and colonise the full 21 days yourself.

## Why This Approach Is Lower Risk

**1. Revenue starts sooner.**
In the standard model, no revenue is generated until Month 3 (after Phase 0 + Phase 1 build and colonisation pipeline fills). In this scenario, revenue starts as soon as Stage 1 blocks arrive — potentially within weeks of the fruiting room opening.

**2. You learn before you commit.**
Running the fruiting room on bought blocks tells you whether the room spec is correct, whether temperature and humidity hold, and whether your customers will actually buy at ฿600/kg — before spending ฿278,000 on the colonisation room and lab.

**3. Capital is released in stages.**
Stage 1 pays back in ~6 months. Those earnings can fund Stage 2 and Stage 3 instead of requiring them to be borrowed or invested upfront.

**4. The supplier relationship has value.**
Earthling Mushroom or a Thai LM substrate supplier who provides your Stage 1 and Stage 2 blocks becomes a natural source of cultivation knowledge and a fallback during startup problems. This is worth more than the premium paid per block in the early stages.
